# 🥇 Gold Layer

The Gold layer presents business-level analytics, data marts, aggregated metrics, and dimensional models (star schemas).

## Key Design Principles
1. **Consumption Ready**: Designed specifically for consumption by BI dashboards (Power BI, Tableau), ad-hoc querying, and reporting.
2. **Dimensional Modeling**: Fact and Dimension tables organized in star/snowflake schemas.
3. **Aggregations & KPI Metrics**: Pre-calculated business KPIs, rollups, and time-series snapshots.

## Folder Structure
- `ddl/`: DDL scripts for Snowflake Gold tables, analytical views, and business data marts.
